# Support

## Get help

This repository does not specify a dedicated support address, issue tracker, or response-time commitment. Use the support channel provided by the distributor that supplied the APK. If the project's hosting service provides an enabled issue tracker, use that for reproducible app problems.

## Before asking

Include the app version, Android version, what you did, what you expected, and what happened. Mention whether notification permission and the relevant Android notification channel are enabled.

Never include your OAuth token, passwords, signing credentials, or unredacted logs. Remove personal information and private Twitch account details from screenshots and diagnostic output.

## Limitations

Monitoring depends on Twitch API availability, network access, Android permissions, and Android background-execution policies. The foreground service polls every 60 seconds while active; WorkManager fallback checks are best effort and may be delayed. The project makes no real-time delivery or response-time guarantee.
